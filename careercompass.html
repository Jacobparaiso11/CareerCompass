About Career Compass

Our vision is to empower students to confidently choose their future careers by giving the them access to clear and reliable guidance. We strive to promote informed and wise decision-making among learners. In the future, we hope this platform will become a helpful and trusted digital tool for career exploration.

Our mission is to provide a simple and interactive career guidance platform that helps students explore different strands and career opportunities. We aim to support learners in choosing a strand that matches their interests, skills, and abilities. Through this website, we want to guide students in making better decision about their academic and career paths.
